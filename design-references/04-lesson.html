Step 1 of 3

What "buying" actually means

When you buy a coin, you're not buying a physical thing — you're trading some of your money for a digital asset. The amount you receive depends on the current price.

On the right, you have $1,000 in play money. Try buying a small amount of Bitcoin and watch what happens.

!
Key idea

1 dollar buys you a small fraction of 1 Bitcoin. Today, $100 ≈ 0.00156 BTC. Tomorrow, the same $100 might buy more or less.

FIN SAYS

Most beginners start with $50–$100. Just enough to feel the price moves without thinking about it too much. You can reset your balance any time.
