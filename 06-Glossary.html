In plain English

The U.S. central bank didn't change interest rates, but hinted they might lower them soon. A big basket of 500 American companies went up 1.3%. Long-term government bonds got slightly cheaper to borrow against.

Why it matters: Lower rates usually mean cheaper loans and happier stock prices. That's why the market went up today.
