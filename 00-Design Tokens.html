Colors

--brand · --ink · --bg · semantic
One bold accent, lots of space. Terracotta carries every CTA, highlight, and focus state. Everything else is a warm ink-on-paper neutral. Semantic colors (success/danger) appear only inside financial UI — never as decoration.

Manrope · 400 / 500 / 600 / 700 / 800
